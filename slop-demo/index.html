RoomBook cleanup steps

The same meeting-room booking app, cleaned up one kind of slop at a time. Each step keeps every earlier fix. Counts are from the served page at 1440px, without the presenter overlay; the change in brackets is against the previous version.

  1. Original

    The vibe-coded starting point: every slop category is present.

    Impeccable 122 findings (104 counted, 18 advisory) · Snyk 8 issues

  2. 01 Pills

    Removed badges, chips and pills with no information; availability is plain coloured text.

    Impeccable 90 (−32) findings (72 counted, 18 advisory) · Snyk 8 (±0) issues

  3. 02 Clutter

    Removed eyebrows, subtitles, stats, logos, testimonials and the CTA band; plain copy.

    Impeccable 39 (−51) findings (33 counted, 6 advisory) · Snyk 8 (±0) issues

  4. 03 Spacing

    One 4/8px spacing scale, aligned room grid, one section rhythm.

    Impeccable 39 (±0) findings (33 counted, 6 advisory) · Snyk 8 (±0) issues

  5. 04 Design system

    Tokens, one font, one button component, one radius scale; no gradients, glass or emoji.

    Impeccable 10 (−29) findings (10 counted, 0 advisory) · Snyk 8 (±0) issues

  6. 05 Contrast

    WCAG AA contrast, 14px/12px minimum text, real buttons and links, labels, focus styles.

    Impeccable 0 (−10) findings (0 counted, 0 advisory) · Snyk 8 (±0) issues

  7. 06 Final MT

    MT (Material) design system from mytGPT: light and dark themes, photo room cards with a quiet status line, two-column booking area.

    Impeccable 1 (+1) findings (1 counted, 0 advisory) · Snyk 8 (±0) issues

Snyk stays about flat on purpose: the code-level slop (eval, XSS, secrets, the overlap bug, fake tests) is left for the code-review demo. Each page has a presenter overlay with numbered change pins and the full reports below its footer; untick "Show changes" to hide it.
